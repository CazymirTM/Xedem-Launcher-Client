# Xedem Launcher

**Simple. Lightweight. Fast.**

Xedem Launcher is a lightweight Minecraft launcher built with one goal in mind: **give you what you need without getting in your way.**

No unnecessary bloat.
No advertisements.
No intrusive promotions.
No overloaded interface.

Just a fast, clean launcher designed to get you into Minecraft as quickly as possible.

## Why Xedem?

Most launchers try to do more and more until the launcher itself becomes heavier than it needs to be. Xedem takes the opposite approach.

### ⚡ Fast Downloads

Download Minecraft versions, Fabric components, mods, and other required files quickly and efficiently, without unnecessary overhead.

### 🪶 Lightweight

Xedem is designed to stay small and responsive. It doesn't need dozens of unnecessary background processes or features you will never use.

### 🚫 No Ads

**No advertisements. No sponsored content. No promotional banners.**

Xedem is built around the experience of the user, not around filling the launcher with ads.

### 🧹 No Bloat

Only the features that actually matter.

Xedem avoids unnecessary services, excessive UI elements, and features that add complexity without adding value.

### 🎯 Simple

Open the launcher, choose what you want to play, and launch.

The interface is designed to be straightforward so you spend less time configuring the launcher and more time playing.

### 🔒 User-Focused

Xedem is designed to remain transparent and predictable. Your launcher should launch your game — it shouldn't constantly try to sell you something.

## The Philosophy

> **A launcher doesn't need to be complicated to be powerful.**

Xedem focuses on the fundamentals:

* Fast startup
* Fast downloads
* Low overhead
* Clean interface
* Reliable Minecraft launching
* Fabric support
* Mod support
* Custom Xedem Client features
* No advertisements
* No unnecessary bloat

Everything else comes second.

---

## Built for Minecraft

Xedem is designed around a modern Fabric-based Minecraft experience, with support for performance-focused mods and **Xedem Client**, our own custom client-side feature system.

The goal is to provide a smooth experience while keeping the launcher itself lightweight and out of the way.
